Privacy Policy

Effective: May 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect account information (email, name), API usage data, and transaction records. We do NOT store document content—all document processing happens locally in your browser.

2. How We Use Your Information

To provide and improve our services, process payments, detect abuse, comply with legal obligations, and communicate with you about your account.

3. Data Security

We use AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. API keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes only. PII is automatically redacted at the edge.

4. Data Retention

Transaction logs are retained for 7 years for compliance. API usage logs are retained for 90 days. You may request data deletion at any time.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, and export your data. Contact privacy@sovereigngrid.com for requests.
